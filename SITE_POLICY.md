# EduSmart Korea site policy

## Brand positioning

- Primary user-facing brand: **TNS유학**.
- Primary product framing: 캐나다 조기유학, 관리형 유학, 가디언형 유학 and individual programme names.
- EduSmart is the **local programme operator / source partner**, not the front-facing brand of this website.
- Do not optimize page titles, H1 headings, hero copy, primary navigation, or primary SEO targeting around the keyword `에듀스마트` by default.
- Mention EduSmart only where its identity is operationally necessary, such as explaining local programme operation and responsibilities, or where there is a specific contractual/compliance reason.
- Consultation and conversion CTAs should prioritize **TNS유학**.

## Visible source policy

- **Do not display source/reference attribution on public EduSmart website pages.**
- Do not add user-visible sections or boxes such as `자료 출처`, `공식 출처`, `참고 자료`, source footnotes, or source-link collections to programme/guide pages.
- Do not expose EduSmart, school, district, or other official links merely for source attribution.
- Continue to verify tuition, programme scope, schedules, school information, admissions details, and other factual claims internally using the latest EduSmart materials and authoritative/official sources before publishing.
- Internal verification records may be kept in GitHub, working notes, or central management configuration, but should not be surfaced as source attribution in the public page UI.
- Search-engine structured data may contain appropriate entity URLs because those are not user-facing source attribution.
- News is currently disabled for this site. If news publishing is enabled in the future, follow the project-wide news-source policy unless a newer site-specific policy is approved.

This policy may be overridden only when there is a specific legal, contractual, compliance, or explicit user-requested reason.
